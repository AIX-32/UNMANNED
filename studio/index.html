STUDIO HELP

  • Camera — RMB drag look around · WASD fly toward the view (R/F up/down) · wheel zoom/dolly · Shift = fast
  • 1 Select — click pick · drag move · arrows slide · PgUp/Dn lift · [ ] rotate · -/+ scale · V dup · X del
  • 2 Place — pick prop/block/entity, aim + click to stamp (keeps going) · rotY slider spins the ghost before you stamp · G toggle snap
  • 3 Terrain — hold click to sculpt · raise/lower/smooth/flatten in panel
  • 4 Route — click to add UGV waypoints · X on a dot deletes it
  • 5 Paint — hold click paints the active ground layer · Alt erases back to grass · add layers in panel
  • 6 Wall — click = vertices (auto-connect) · Enter/RMB finishes the wall · Backspace drops last vertex · walls are invisible blockers in-game (block player + UGVs)
  • 7 Story — floating windows: intro sections (typed on spawn), cutscene camera path (add points where you fly, ▶ preview), text zones (walk into a ring → subtitle)
  • 8 Greenery — floating window: paint forests. pick trees/bushes/both, how many per click, brush radius, tree & bush size ranges. Left-click stamps a spaced grouping so canopies don't overlap
  • 0 Grass — floating window: upload a transparent sprite (grass tuft / wheat stalk), hold LMB + draw to paint billboard grass. Each painted point spawns 2-6 crossed billboard pairs, randomly rotated — all blades render as one merged mesh, so big fields stay fast
  • Ground — pick or upload a base-ground tile texture + tile size for the map (default is grass); the paint tool still draws on top of it
  • Pixelizer — image → downscale + posterize + Bayer dither → usable block texture
  • Ctrl+Z undo · autosave · ▶ Play opens the map in-game
